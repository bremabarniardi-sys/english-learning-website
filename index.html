Complete English Learning Materials

1. Grammar

Grammar is a set of rules used to arrange words into correct sentences.


Simple Present Tense:

It is used to express habits, facts, and daily activities.


Formula:

Subject + Verb 1 + Object


Examples:

  • I study English every day.
  • She drinks milk every morning.
  • They play football on Sunday.

2. Speaking

Speaking is the ability to communicate fluently in English.


Conversation Example:

A : Hello, how are you?
B : I am fine, thank you.


Speaking Tips:

  • Practice speaking every day
  • Listen to English songs
  • Watch English movies
  • Speak confidently without fear

3. Vocabulary

Vocabulary is a collection of English words.


Vocabulary Examples:

  • Beautiful = Pretty
  • School = Place to study
  • Teacher = Educator
  • Book = Reading material
  • Friend = Companion

4. Reading

Reading is the ability to read and understand English texts.


Text Example:

My name is Andi. I am a student. I go to school every day. I like learning English because it is fun.


Meaning:

The text explains about a student who enjoys learning English every day.

5. Writing

Writing is the ability to express ideas in written English.


Example:

"I love my family because they always support me."

6. Listening

Listening is the ability to understand spoken English conversations.


Listening Tips:

  • Listen to English songs
  • Watch movies without subtitles
  • Practice listening to English podcasts
